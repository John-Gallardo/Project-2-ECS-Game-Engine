We implemented the platformer as close to the given spec as we could. What basically happens is that the engine is created in main, and:
1. Sounds are loaded
2. Maps are loaded (sky first so its sprites are drawn BEHIND the world)
3. Bonus star is placed
4. Player is placed & given components
5. Score component is set up
6. Camera is placed
7. Systems are added to the system manager
and the engine runs.

Notes about implementation:
- **Wall Jump**: we implemented the wall jump so that while pressing in the direction of the wall
and press jump you can climb the wall.
- **Player Sprite Size**: we used 64x64 screen width and height for the player size
- **Camera Components**: CameraFollowerComponent.h and CameraShakeComponent.h were split out of
CameraComponent.h so every component has its own header. The structs inside keep their original
names (CameraFollower and CameraShake).
- **Location Components**: MovementComponent.h, GravityComponent.h and RespawnComponent.h were split
out of LocationComponent.h for the same reason. Their structs are unchanged.

Movement controls:
A/left = move left
D/right = move right
Z/W/Space/Up = jump (let go early for a lower jump)
C/Left Shift = dash

Debug keys:
F1 = pause
F2 = step one frame
F3 = toggle collision boxes
